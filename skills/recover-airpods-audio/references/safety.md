# Safety boundary

## Required

- Require a direct click or terminal confirmation before playback.
- Require the AirPods to be out of the user's ears.
- Ask the user to place the speaker meshes downward on a clean, dry cloth.
- Keep gain and duration bounded in code.
- Stop immediately if an AirPod becomes warm, rattles sharply, or causes
  discomfort.

## Not supported

- Guaranteed removal of liquid
- Firmware installation or forced AirPods updates
- Repairing a damaged speaker, battery, microphone, or charging contact
- Reliable browser-level verification of the operating-system output device

## Privacy

Do not collect, print, save, or publish:

- Bluetooth MAC addresses
- pairing databases or preference files
- environment variables
- local usernames or absolute home paths
- diagnostic logs tied to a specific person or device
