What did you hear?

Before any audio test

Dry first. Test second.

Water resistance is not waterproofing. Give the hardware time to dry before checking the sound.

  1. 1

    Wipe dry. Use a soft, dry, lint-free cloth.

  2. 2

    Wait at least two hours. Do not use the AirPods or return them to the charging case before they are fully dry.

  3. 3

    Avoid forced drying. Do not use heat or compressed air.

Based on Apple’s current water-exposure guidance .

What the result tells you

The symptom is the diagnostic.

  1. 01

    Strange only in Noise Control

    The transparency or ANC microphone and vent are more likely than the speaker driver.

  2. 02

    Quiet in every mode

    Check the speaker mesh, charging contact, battery level, and left/right balance.

  3. 03

    Still crackling after drying

    Stop testing. Persistent distortion can indicate a damaged microphone or driver that needs service.

Common questions

Wet, damaged, or one-sided AirPods

Can a website dry wet AirPods?

No website can guarantee liquid removal or hardware repair. Dry the AirPods completely for at least two hours first. This page can then play a bounded low-frequency pulse that may move small droplets away from the speaker mesh.

What should I do if one AirPod stopped working after getting wet?

Let both AirPods dry completely for at least two hours, confirm each one is charging, then run the left and right test. Persistent silence or distortion may require cleaning, a reset, or hardware service.

Does this update AirPods firmware?

No. AirPods firmware updates are controlled by Apple devices. This tool only runs local audio tests and explains what the result means.

Can this website show live AirPods battery percentage?

No normal browser can read AirPods battery status from macOS or iOS. The optional Mac command above requests a current snapshot and prints only left, right, and case percentages that macOS exposes.

Does this work with every AirPods generation?

The moisture and left/right audio tests work with any AirPods generation selected as your audio output. The optional Mac battery snapshot is best-effort because the fields exposed by macOS vary by hardware and system version.

Stop if an AirPod becomes warm, rattles sharply, or causes discomfort. Never use heat, compressed air, or sharp tools.
